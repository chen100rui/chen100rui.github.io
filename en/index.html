About

Who I am

I'm a product manager, and now a founder too. In December 2025 I left iFLYTEK and co-founded Hibot with my partners. Our first product is the Hibot S50, an AI portable Bluetooth speaker that plays songs you request by voice. At iFLYTEK I was a Senior Product Manager. My main product was the iFLYTEK Smart Recorder SR series, and I also owned the iFLYTEK SayLeader S8 meeting transcriber series end to end, from project kickoff to sales.

I've been a product manager since 2011. I've worked on navigation apps (Navidog), dash cams (Goluk, 360 Dash Cam), connected cars (LeEco Car Connect) and Android launchers (360 Launcher, Hola Launcher), as well as the desktop website for Huajiao Live, a livestreaming platform. Before that, from 2008 to 2011, I did technical support for map data at Beijing Chengji Gaoke. That is where my career began.

Many of the products I've worked on combine hardware and software: dash cams, voice recorders, meeting transcribers. The device and its phone, web and PC software all have to be thought through together. Besides being a product manager and a founder, I also see myself as a problem solver. Outside work, I'm a dad, and I've always thought of myself as a lifelong learner.

Hibot S50 AI portable Bluetooth speaker

The first product from the startup my partners and I founded. Press the Hibot button and say a song's name to play it. Songs are stored on a microSD (TF) card, so voice requests work even offline.
